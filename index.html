About Me

I’m Amelia, a data analyst who treats numbers like a language. Whether I’m building dashboards, exploring customer trends, or experimenting with machine learning, I’m always looking for patterns that explain what’s happening and why.

My work sits at the intersection of analytics and problem-solving. I enjoy taking messy questions, finding the right data to answer them, and creating reports, dashboards, and tools that make information easier to understand and use. I’ve worked with SQL, Tableau, Snowflake, Salesforce, and Python to analyze data and build solutions that support better decisions.

Outside of analytics, I’m usually playing instruments, golfing, hiking, or learning something new just because I’m curious. I’ve always enjoyed the process of figuring things out, whether that’s a dataset, a song, or a new skill.
